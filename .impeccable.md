## Design Context

### Users

Funtime serves casual NFL pick'em players and league admins. In the renewal
flow, an admin is deciding whom to invite back for the next season and needs
quick, trustworthy context without slowing down the invitation task.

### Brand Personality

Warm, clear, and game-day friendly. The product should feel practical and
inclusive for football fans of any age, with plain language that says exactly
what will happen.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the established dark-first Funtime interface: restrained surfaces,
green primary actions, and amber only for meaningful caution states. A missed
pick should read as useful context, not a punitive or alarming judgement.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the action and its outcome.
2. Keep admin decisions easy to scan and easy to reverse.
3. Use warnings sparingly, with both an icon and concise explanatory text.
4. Preserve a warm, game-day tone; clarity matters more than cleverness.
5. Keep essential information and controls usable on narrow screens.
